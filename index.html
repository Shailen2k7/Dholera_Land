01 · At a glance

What we are building, in one page

The land was bought at ₹4,000 raw and only 64% of it can be sold. Profit depends on price discipline, sales pace and lean development. Every plot is sold directly, with no dealer commission in this plan. The full profit and loss is revealed at the end, once the last plot is sold.
02 · Location

Close to everything Dholera is building

Under a kilometre from the Ahmedabad–Dholera Expressway, in the Free Zone, with the airport, the SIR boundary and the Lothal heritage complex all within a short drive.

03 · Master plan

A gated township around a tree-lined boulevard

Nine rows of plots on 9 m roads, a 15 m entry boulevard, green belts with a jogging track along both side walls, and a front zone for the clubhouse, pool, garden and sports court.
